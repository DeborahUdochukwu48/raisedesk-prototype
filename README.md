# RaiseDesk prototype

A clickable concept for [RaiseDesk](https://raisedesk.io), the fundraising operating platform for founders. Built by **Amajuoyi Udochukwu Deborah**, Senior Product Designer & Product Manager.

**Live prototype:** https://deborahudochukwu48.github.io/raisedesk-prototype/

The idea in one line: the agent prepares the work, the founder decides, and every step is recorded.

![Approvals screen](screenshots/approvals.png)

## What you can click through

| Screen | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Your story** | The founder describes the company in their own words. The agent separates verified facts from assumptions, and the founder confirms the assumptions. |
| **Discover** | Investors ranked by stage, sector, geography and network. Every match shows its public evidence, its assumptions, the best route (warm intro or direct) and any conflict that needs the founder's call. |
| **Approvals** | Each draft lists the claims behind it. Approve stays locked until open claims are confirmed or removed. After approval, the record shows who approved, when, which route, and the exact text. |
| **Inbox** | A reply becomes a task for the founder, with talking points and history. A decline ends the sequence. Silence gets a review date. |
| **This week** | Work done and work waiting, an owner for every item, and the bottleneck to clear first. |

### Try this path

1. Open **Approvals**. Harbourline Capital has an open claim, so Approve is locked.
2. Under *Claims in this message*, click **Still true** (or **Remove from message**).
3. Click **Approve and send** and see the approval record.
4. Switch the route between **Warm intro** and **Direct message**, or click **Edit** to change the text.
5. Go to **Inbox** and open each conversation to see a reply, a decline and a silence.

Each screen has its own link, for example `#approvals` or `#inbox`.

## Built from RaiseDesk's own workflow

The flow follows the outreach process RaiseDesk describes publicly: prepare the approach and separate facts from assumptions, choose the route, get founder approval with a record of what was sent, handle replies as tasks, follow up on silence only with a useful update, and track progress weekly.
